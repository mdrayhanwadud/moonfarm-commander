# Child playtest sheet (3 children aged 7 to 12)

## Privacy rules (the submission rules ban names, voices and likenesses of anyone under 18)
- Get a parent's permission first.
- Record NO names, photos, videos or voice recordings in anything you submit. Write "Child A, age 9".
- Tell the child to use made-up astronaut names in the game.
- Never put children's quotes with identifying details into the form, demo or screenshots.

## How
1. Give the child the phone. Say only: "Try this game and tell me what you think. I won't help."
2. Watch 5 to 8 minutes. Do not explain. Note where they hesitate or ask "what do I do?"
3. At the end, tap ℹ️ then 📋 Playtest notes then Copy. Paste it into your notes.
4. Ask the 4 questions.

## Questions
1. What were you trying to do? (checks understanding)
2. What was the most fun part?
3. What did the shield / battery / greenhouse do? (checks learning)
4. What would you change?

## Use it
Write one summary sentence for the form, for example "3 children aged 8 to 11 played for 5 minutes; all 3 understood the goal within 2 minutes."
